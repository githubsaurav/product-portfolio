CONVERSATIONAL ANALYTICS

From waiting for data
to asking a question.

Led discovery and delivery of a self-service Data AI Assistant, translating 20+ user interviews into a product spanning five risk datasets.

30 min → <1 minQuery turnaround
10×Growth in daily users · 10 → 100
The product decision +

Replace a two-stage question-refinement flow with one conversation. Balance answer quality, latency, and token cost while making dataset onboarding reusable. New integrations went from 12 weeks to 4 weeks.

DOCUMENT INTELLIGENCE

Less manual extraction.
More time for analysis.

Took a document AI proof of concept through to production, helping analysts extract 80+ financial metrics from financial statements and prospectuses.

90%Less time per document · 5 hr → 30 min
75%Lower cost vs. the group solution
The product decision +

Make the build-vs-buy decision on workflow fit and economics. Optimize parsing and token usage, retain analyst review, and connect extracted metrics to downstream risk ratings. The defined extraction benchmark averaged 95% accuracy.

03 / HOW I WORK

Start curious.
Stay practical.

I like the space between a messy problem
and something someone can actually use.

01 ↗

Find the real friction.

Understand the workflow, the user, and the decision. Separate a symptom from the problem worth solving.

02 ↗

Make the trade-offs.

Choose what matters first. Weigh user value against effort, accuracy, cost, and the consequences of being wrong.

03 ↗

Build to learn.

Put something tangible in people’s hands. Use a working prototype to make assumptions visible and testable.

04 ↗

Close the loop.

Look beyond the launch. Learn from adoption, outcomes, and feedback to decide what should happen next.
